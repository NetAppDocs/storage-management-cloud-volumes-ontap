= Convert NIS subscriptions to standalone Cloud Volumes ONTAP subscriptions in AWS
:hardbreaks:
:icons: font
:imagesdir: ./media/

[.lead]
Move your Cloud Volumes ONTAP consumption from the NetApp Intelligent Services (NIS) marketplace listing to the Cloud Volumes ONTAP standalone listing (NSL), the preferred procurement path for Cloud Volumes ONTAP.

The standalone listing is the preferred procurement path for Cloud Volumes ONTAP, providing a dedicated subscription model. Customers consuming Backup and other NetApp Data Services along with the standalone listing must continue to consume those services through NIS using the supported https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/concept-billing-preferences.html#flexible-billing-for-cloud-volumes-ontap-and-data-services-in-aws[multiple subscription model in AWS^].

A single NetApp Console account can have several marketplace subscriptions at a time. The system routes charges according to your configured billing preferences. The following combinations are supported:

[cols=2*,options="header"]
|===
| Service
| Listing

| Cloud Volumes ONTAP Essentials
| NSL

| NetApp Backup and Recovery
| NIS

| NetApp Console data services
| NIS

| Cloud Volumes ONTAP annual contract
| NSL

| Cloud Volumes ONTAP pay-as-you-go or PAYGO
| NSL PAYGO
|===

.Before you begin
Review your charging needs to determine which scenario applies to you:

* Your current marketplace subscriptions
* Your current charging method, which can be:
** Cloud Volumes ONTAP Essentials
** Cloud Volumes ONTAP Professional
* Backup usage
* Data services consumption
* The status of your existing marketplace contract
* The AWS Marketplace listing that is currently associated with your Console account

== Convert Cloud Volumes ONTAP NIS subscriptions with Essentials license

Use this scenario if you currently have an NIS subscription with Cloud Volumes ONTAP Essentials license and no dependency on Backup and Recovery. After conversion, you have an NSL subscription with Cloud Volumes ONTAP Essentials license.

=== Convert a PAYGO subscription

You have a CLoud Volumes ONTAP NIS PAYGO subscription before conversion.

.Steps
. From AWS Marketplace, subscribe to Cloud Volumes ONTAP NSL subscription with the PAYGO option, and associate the NSL subscription with your Console account. Refer to link:task-onboard-cvo-aws-marketplace.html[Deploy Cloud Volumes ONTAP using the standalone listing on AWS Marketplace].
. On the Console, go to *Administration > Licenses and subscriptions* and verify that the correct subscription is preferred for Cloud Volumes ONTAP billing. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-billing-preferences.html#set-billing-preferences[Licenses and subscriptions: Set billing preferences^].
. Verify the charging and entitlement status.
. Remove the NIS subscription if you no longer need it. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-subscriptions.html[Licenses and subscriptions: Remove a subscription^].

=== Convert an annual contract or private offer

You have a Cloud Volumes ONTAP NIS annual contract before conversion.

.Steps
. Obtain a replacement or amendment private offer for Cloud Volumes ONTAP through the NSL listing. Contact your NetApp accounts team.
. Accept the new offer and activate the new subscription, and associate the NSL subscription with your Console account. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-private-aws.html#accept-the-private-offer-in-the-aws-marketplace-portal[Licenses and subscriptions: Accept and configure an AWS Marketplace private offer ^].
. Go to *Administration > Licenses and subscriptions* and verify that the correct subscription is preferred for Cloud Volumes ONTAP billing. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-billing-preferences.html#set-billing-preferences[Licenses and subscriptions: Set billing preferences^].
. Verify the charging and entitlement status.
. Remove the NIS subscription if you no longer need it. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-subscriptions.html[Licenses and subscriptions: Remove a subscription^].

== Convert Cloud Volumes ONTAP Essentials subscriptions that use Backup

Use this scenario if you currently have an NIS subscription with Cloud Volumes ONTAP Essentials license, and have Backup and Recovery or any other data services enabled. After you convert, you use an NSL subscription for Cloud Volumes ONTAP and an NIS subscription for Backup and Recovery.

=== Convert a PAYGO subscription

You have a Cloud Volumes ONTAP NIS PAYGO subscription before conversion.

.Steps
. Subscribe to the NSL PAYGO offering for Cloud Volumes ONTAP from the AWS Marketplace. Refer to link:task-onboard-cvo-aws-marketplace.html[Deploy Cloud Volumes ONTAP using the standalone listing on AWS Marketplace].
. Keep your NIS subscription for Backup and Recovery, or add one if you don't have one.
. Associate both subscriptions with the same Console account. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-subscriptions.html[Licenses and subscriptions: Associate a subscription with a console account^].
. Verify the subscription associations and billing behavior. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-billing-preferences.html#verify-billing-preferences[Licenses and subscriptions: Verify billing preferences^].
. Verify that Cloud Volumes ONTAP charges go through the NSL subscription and that data services charges go through the NIS subscription. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/concept-billing-preferences.html#flexible-billing-for-cloud-volumes-ontap-and-data-services-in-aws[Licenses and subscriptions: Flexible billing for Cloud Volumes ONTAP and data services in AWS^].

=== Convert an annual contract or private offer

You have a Cloud Volumes ONTAP NIS annual contract or private offer before conversion.

.Steps
. Obtain a replacement or amendment private offer for Cloud Volumes ONTAP through the NSL listing. Contact your NetApp accounts team.
. Obtain or retain a valid NIS subscription for Backup and Recovery in one of the following ways:
** Through an NIS PAYGO subscription
** Through a new or amended NIS private offer that covers the Backup and Recovery data services
. Accept the required offers and activate the subscriptions.
. Associate both subscriptions with the same Console account. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-subscriptions.html[Licenses and subscriptions: Associate a subscription with a console account^].
. Verify the subscription associations and billing behavior. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-billing-preferences.html#verify-billing-preferences[Licenses and subscriptions: Verify billing preferences^].
. Verify that Cloud Volumes ONTAP charges go through the NSL subscription and that data services charges go through the NIS subscription. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/concept-billing-preferences.html#flexible-billing-for-cloud-volumes-ontap-and-data-services-in-aws[Licenses and subscriptions: Flexible billing for Cloud Volumes ONTAP and data services in AWS^].


.Result
You have a supported https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/concept-billing-preferences.html#flexible-billing-for-cloud-volumes-ontap-and-data-services-in-aws[multi-subscription configuration^].

== Convert Cloud Volumes ONTAP Professional subscriptions without data services

If you have a Cloud Volumes ONTAP NIS subscription with a Professional license and don't actively use or plan to use Backup and Recovery or any other data services, you must convert your charging method from Professional to Essentials. The preferred target state is a Cloud Volumes ONTAP NSL subscription with an Essentials license.

You must complete the conversion from Professional to Essentials before the NIS subscription expires or before you remove it. If you don't convert your charging method:

* Your Cloud Volumes ONTAP system continues to be charged through the existing NIS Professional license.
* You won't have a valid Professional billing path to continue charging your Cloud Volumes ONTAP usage. 
* The conversion workflow might become unavailable after the NIS subscription expires.


=== Convert a PAYGO subscription

.Steps
. Change the charging method from Professional to Essentials. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-capacity-licenses.html#change-charging-methods[Licenses and subscriptions: Change charging methods^].
. Subscribe to the NSL PAYGO offering for Cloud Volumes ONTAP from the AWS Marketplace. Refer to link:task-onboard-cvo-aws-marketplace.html[Deploy Cloud Volumes ONTAP using the standalone listing on AWS Marketplace].
. Associate the NSL subscription with your Console account. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-subscriptions.html[Licenses and subscriptions: Associate a subscription with a console account^].
. Verify the subscription preferences and charging. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/concept-billing-preferences.html#flexible-billing-for-cloud-volumes-ontap-and-data-services-in-aws[Licenses and subscriptions: Flexible billing for Cloud Volumes ONTAP and data services in AWS^].
. Remove the NIS subscription if you no longer need it. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-subscriptions.html[Licenses and subscriptions: Remove a subscription^].
=== Convert an annual contract or private offer

.Steps
. Obtain a replacement or amendment private offer for Cloud Volumes ONTAP Essentials through NSL.
. Accept the new offer and activate the subscription.
. Change the charging method from Professional to Essentials. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-capacity-licenses.html#change-charging-methods[Licenses and subscriptions: Change charging methods^].
. Associate the NSL subscription with your Console account. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-subscriptions.html[Licenses and subscriptions: Associate a subscription with a console account^].
. Verify the subscription preferences and charging. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/concept-billing-preferences.html#flexible-billing-for-cloud-volumes-ontap-and-data-services-in-aws[Licenses and subscriptions: Flexible billing for Cloud Volumes ONTAP and data services in AWS^].
. Remove the NIS subscription if you no longer need it. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-subscriptions.html[Licenses and subscriptions: Remove a subscription^].

== Troubleshooting

Learn what you need to do if you encounter the following issues:

=== You have a Cloud Volumes ONTAP Professional license

Professional to Essentials is the only supported conversion if you have a Cloud Volumes ONTAP subscription and want to convert to an NSL listing.
You must change the charging method from Professional to Essentials if you don't want to continue using Professional features and want to move to Essentials:

https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-capacity-licenses.html#change-charging-methods[Licenses and subscriptions: Change charging methods^].

=== Environment still uses Professional after you purchase NSL

This behavior is expected. NSL supports only Essentials. Do one of the following:

* Change the charging method from Professional to Essentials.
* Continue to use Professional through NIS.

=== You use Backup and want to move to NSL

Keep Backup on NIS and use the multi-subscription model. Use NSL for Cloud Volumes ONTAP and NIS for Backup. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-billing-preferences.html[Licenses and subscriptions: Manage billing preferences^].
