= Convert NIS subscriptions to standalone Cloud Volumes ONTAP subscriptions in AWS
:hardbreaks:
:icons: font
:imagesdir: ./media/

[.lead]
Move your Cloud Volumes ONTAP consumption from the NetApp Intelligent Services (NIS) listing to the Cloud Volumes ONTAP standalone listing (NSL) on AWS Marketplace.

The standalone listing is the preferred procurement path for Cloud Volumes ONTAP from the AWS Marketplace, providing a dedicated subscription model. The standalone listing supports only the Cloud Volumes ONTAP Essentials license. If you want to use the Cloud Volumes ONTAP Professional license, NetApp Backup and Recovery, or other NetApp Data Services, you must continue to use NIS subscriptions for those services. If you want to use Cloud Volumes ONTAP Essentials Primary and Essentials Secondary licenses with data services, you can use both NSL and NIS listings in the supported https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/concept-billing-preferences.html#flexible-billing-for-cloud-volumes-ontap-and-data-services-in-aws[multiple subscription model in AWS^].

A single NetApp Console account can have several marketplace subscriptions at a time. If you have an Essentials license for Cloud Volumes ONTAP, the system automatically routes your consumption charges through NSL, unless configured otherwise in your Billing Preferences. You can use these combinations:

|===
| Service
| Listing

a| Cloud Volumes ONTAP Essentials Primary and Essentials Secondary licenses
a| NSL with pay-as-you-go or PAYGO or annual contract

a| Cloud Volumes ONTAP Professional license 
NetApp Backup and Recovery
Disaster Recovery 
Ransomware Resilience 
a| NIS with PAYGO or annual contract
a| Cloud Volumes ONTAP Essentials Primary and Backup and Recovery or other data services
a| NSL for Cloud Volumes ONTAP Essentials Primary, and NIS for Backup and Recovery or data services

|===

.Before you begin
Review your existing offering and marketplace subscription, and estimate your future usage and license requirements. Consider the following:

* Your current Cloud Volumes ONTAP license or charging method, which can be:
** Cloud Volumes ONTAP Essentials
** Cloud Volumes ONTAP Professional
* Requirements for Backup and Recovery or any other data services
* The status of your existing NIS or NSL contract
* The AWS Marketplace listing that is currently associated with your Console account

== Convert Cloud Volumes ONTAP NIS subscriptions with an Essentials license

Use this scenario if you have an NIS PAYGO or annual subscription with a Cloud Volumes ONTAP Essentials license and you don't use Backup and Recovery or any other data services. After conversion, you have an NSL PAYGO or annual subscription with a Cloud Volumes ONTAP Essentials license.

=== Convert a PAYGO subscription

You have a Cloud Volumes ONTAP NIS PAYGO subscription before conversion.

.Steps
. From AWS Marketplace, subscribe to the Cloud Volumes ONTAP NSL PAYGO subscription and associate the NSL subscription with your Console account. Refer to link:task-onboard-cvo-aws-marketplace.html[Deploy Cloud Volumes ONTAP using the standalone listing on AWS Marketplace].
. On the Console, go to *Administration > Licenses and subscriptions* and verify that the correct subscription is preferred for Cloud Volumes ONTAP billing. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-billing-preferences.html#set-billing-preferences[Licenses and subscriptions: Set billing preferences^].
. Verify the charging and entitlement status.
. Remove the NIS subscription if you no longer need it. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-subscriptions.html[Licenses and subscriptions: Remove a subscription^].

=== Convert an annual contract or private offer

You have a Cloud Volumes ONTAP NIS annual contract before conversion.

.Steps
. Obtain a replacement or amendment private offer for Cloud Volumes ONTAP through the NSL listing. Contact your NetApp accounts team.
. Accept the new offer, activate the new subscription, and associate the NSL subscription with your Console account. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-private-aws.html#accept-the-private-offer-in-the-aws-marketplace-portal[Licenses and subscriptions: Accept and configure an AWS Marketplace private offer ^].
. Go to *Administration > Licenses and subscriptions* and verify that the correct subscription is preferred for Cloud Volumes ONTAP billing. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-billing-preferences.html#set-billing-preferences[Licenses and subscriptions: Set billing preferences^].
. Verify the charging and billing preferences.
. Remove the NIS subscription if you no longer need it. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-subscriptions.html[Licenses and subscriptions: Remove a subscription^].

== Convert Cloud Volumes ONTAP Essentials subscriptions that use Backup

Use this scenario if you have an NIS PAYGO or annual subscription with a Cloud Volumes ONTAP Essentials license and have Backup and Recovery or any other data services enabled. After you convert, you use an NSL PAYGO or annual subscription for Cloud Volumes ONTAP and an NIS subscription for Backup and Recovery or data services.

=== Convert a PAYGO subscription

You have a Cloud Volumes ONTAP NIS PAYGO subscription before conversion.

.Steps
. Subscribe to the NSL PAYGO offering for Cloud Volumes ONTAP from the AWS Marketplace. Refer to link:task-onboard-cvo-aws-marketplace.html[Deploy Cloud Volumes ONTAP using the standalone listing on AWS Marketplace].
. Keep your NIS subscription for Backup and Recovery, or add one if you don't have one.
. Associate both subscriptions with the same Console account. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-subscriptions.html[Licenses and subscriptions: Associate a subscription with a console account^].
. Verify the charging and billing preferences. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-billing-preferences.html#verify-billing-preferences[Licenses and subscriptions: Verify billing preferences^].
. Verify that Cloud Volumes ONTAP charges go through the NSL subscription and that data services charges go through the NIS subscription. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/concept-billing-preferences.html#flexible-billing-for-cloud-volumes-ontap-and-data-services-in-aws[Licenses and subscriptions: Flexible billing for Cloud Volumes ONTAP and data services in AWS^].

=== Convert an annual contract or private offer

You have a Cloud Volumes ONTAP NIS annual contract or private offer before conversion.

.Steps
. Obtain a replacement or amendment private offer for Cloud Volumes ONTAP through the NSL listing. Contact your NetApp accounts team.
. Obtain or retain a valid NIS subscription for Backup and Recovery in one of the following ways:
** Through an NIS PAYGO subscription
** Through a new or amended NIS private offer that covers Backup and Recovery or other data services
. Accept the new offer, activate the new subscription, and associate the NSL subscription with your Console account. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-private-aws.html#accept-the-private-offer-in-the-aws-marketplace-portal[Licenses and subscriptions: Accept and configure an AWS Marketplace private offer ^].
. Associate both subscriptions with the same Console account. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-subscriptions.html[Licenses and subscriptions: Associate a subscription with a console account^].
. Verify the charging and billing preferences. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-billing-preferences.html#verify-billing-preferences[Licenses and subscriptions: Verify billing preferences^].
. Verify that Cloud Volumes ONTAP charges go through the NSL subscription and that data services charges go through the NIS subscription. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/concept-billing-preferences.html#flexible-billing-for-cloud-volumes-ontap-and-data-services-in-aws[Licenses and subscriptions: Flexible billing for Cloud Volumes ONTAP and data services in AWS^].


.Result
You have a supported https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/concept-billing-preferences.html#flexible-billing-for-cloud-volumes-ontap-and-data-services-in-aws[multi-subscription configuration^].

== Convert Cloud Volumes ONTAP Professional subscriptions without data services

If you have a Cloud Volumes ONTAP NIS subscription with a Professional license and don't actively use or plan to use Backup and Recovery or any other data services, you must convert your charging method from Professional to Essentials. The preferred target state is a Cloud Volumes ONTAP NSL subscription with an Essentials license.

[NOTE]
You must complete the conversion from Professional to Essentials before the NIS subscription expires or before you remove it. 

If you don't convert your charging method:

* Your Cloud Volumes ONTAP system continues to be charged through the existing NIS Professional license.
* You won't have a valid Professional billing path to continue charging your Cloud Volumes ONTAP usage. 
* The conversion workflow might become unavailable after the NIS subscription expires.


=== Convert a Professional package with PAYGO subscription

.Steps
. Change the charging method from Professional to Essentials. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-capacity-licenses.html#change-charging-methods[Licenses and subscriptions: Change charging methods^].
. Subscribe to the NSL PAYGO offering for Cloud Volumes ONTAP from the AWS Marketplace. Refer to link:task-onboard-cvo-aws-marketplace.html[Deploy Cloud Volumes ONTAP using the standalone listing on AWS Marketplace].
. Associate the NSL subscription with your Console account. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-subscriptions.html[Licenses and subscriptions: Associate a subscription with a console account^].
. Verify the charging and billing preferences. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/concept-billing-preferences.html#flexible-billing-for-cloud-volumes-ontap-and-data-services-in-aws[Licenses and subscriptions: Flexible billing for Cloud Volumes ONTAP and data services in AWS^].
. Remove the NIS subscription if you no longer need it. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-subscriptions.html[Licenses and subscriptions: Remove a subscription^].

=== Convert an annual contract or private offer

.Steps
. Obtain a replacement or amendment private offer for Cloud Volumes ONTAP Essentials through NSL. Contact your NetApp accounts team.
. Accept the new offer, activate the new subscription, and associate the NSL subscription with your Console account. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-private-aws.html#accept-the-private-offer-in-the-aws-marketplace-portal[Licenses and subscriptions: Accept and configure an AWS Marketplace private offer ^].
. Change the charging method from Professional to Essentials. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-capacity-licenses.html#change-charging-methods[Licenses and subscriptions: Change charging methods^].
. Associate the NSL subscription with your Console account. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-subscriptions.html[Licenses and subscriptions: Associate a subscription with a console account^].
. Verify the charging and billing preferences. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/concept-billing-preferences.html#flexible-billing-for-cloud-volumes-ontap-and-data-services-in-aws[Licenses and subscriptions: Flexible billing for Cloud Volumes ONTAP and data services in AWS^].
. Remove the NIS subscription if you no longer need it. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-subscriptions.html[Licenses and subscriptions: Remove a subscription^].

== Troubleshooting

Learn what you need to do if you encounter the following issues:

=== You have a Cloud Volumes ONTAP Professional package

Professional to Essentials is the only supported conversion option if you have a Cloud Volumes ONTAP subscription and want to convert to NSL.
To stop using Professional features and move to Essentials, you must change the charging method from Professional to Essentials:

https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-capacity-licenses.html#change-charging-methods[Licenses and subscriptions: Change charging methods^].

=== Systems use Professional packages even after NSL subscription

This behavior is expected. NSL supports only the Essentials licensing package. Do one of the following:

* Change the charging method from Professional to Essentials. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-capacity-licenses.html#change-charging-methods[Licenses and subscriptions: Change charging methods^].
* Continue to bill against the Professional package through the NIS subscription.

=== You use Backup and Recovery or data services and want to move to NSL

Keep using the NIS subscription for Backup and Recovery or data services, and use the NSL subscription for Cloud Volumes ONTAP in a multi-subscription model. Refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-licenses-subscriptions/task-manage-billing-preferences.html[Licenses and subscriptions: Manage billing preferences^].
